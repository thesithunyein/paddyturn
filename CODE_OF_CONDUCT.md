# Code of conduct

Treat contributors, operators, and reviewers with respect. Give specific feedback about the work, welcome questions, and make room for people with different experience and backgrounds.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and sharing someone’s private information without permission are unacceptable.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where people represent PaddyTurn.

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer. Include enough context to review the incident and share only information relevant to the report. Maintainers may remove harmful content, restrict participation, or take other action appropriate to the situation.
